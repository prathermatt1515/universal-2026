📋 Park Tips — Friday

→
Express is single-use per ride. One tap each. Spend on long-wait rides, walk standby on short ones. Never burn a tap on a walk-on.
→
Bank then spend. Ride easy stuff at standby in the quiet morning. Cash the Express tap in the afternoon when that same line is 60–90 minutes.
→
If a ride goes down — check the Universal app for status. If Mine-Cart or Battle at the Ministry is down, redirect that Express tap to a re-ride on Stardust Racers or Monsters Unchained. Don't leave taps unused.
→
The Universal app is our dashboard. Download before we leave the hotel. Live wait times, Express status, and mobile food orders all live there.
→
Split up if needed. We don't all have to ride together. Two smaller groups can cover more ground — agree on a meet point and check the app.
→
Constellation Carousel at night. The park after dark from Celestial Park is the best photo of the trip. Walk-on at close — no Express needed.

📋 Park Tips — Saturday

→
Arrival is our only edge today. No Express — being at Islands 45 min before open is the single biggest advantage we have. Don't be late to the parking structure.
→
Single rider is our free Express. Skip it on Hagrid's and VelociCoaster if standby is under ~45 min. Use it on everything else.
→
Hogwarts Express shows different scenes each direction. King's Cross to Hogsmeade is one story; Hogsmeade back is another. Ride it both ways if we can.
→
Two rides are closed all of 2026. Rip Ride Rockit is permanently demolished. Jurassic Park River Adventure is closed until November. Our coasters are VelociCoaster and Hulk — both great.
→
Dudley Do-Right strategy. Don't ride it before lunch. Save it for late afternoon when we're hot and don't mind getting soaked. Wear sandals or shoes you're OK with getting wet.
→
Re-ride window. Waits fall hard in the final 60–90 min. Hogwarts Express back to Islands late afternoon for re-rides. Hagrid's posted "90 min" at night often runs ~60.
→
Mobile order is mandatory. Open the Universal app while in a ride line, place our food order, pick it up on the way out. Never stand in a walk-up food queue on a Saturday in June.
